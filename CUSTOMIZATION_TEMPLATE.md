# Quick Customization Template

This file shows exactly what to replace in `index.html` with your actual information.

## Replace These Placeholders:

### Header Section
```
[Your Field] → Your actual field of study
[Your University/Institution] → Your actual university
your.email@university.edu → Your actual email
yourusername → Your actual social media usernames
yourid → Your actual Google Scholar ID
```

### About Section
```
[Advisor Name] → Your actual advisor's name
[Department/Lab Name] → Your actual department or lab
[University Name] → Your university name
[your research area] → Your research area
[specific research interests] → Your specific interests
[dissertation topic] → Your dissertation topic
[other research projects/topics] → Other projects
[Previous Degree] → Your previous degree
[Previous University] → Your previous university
[relevant background information] → Your background
```

### Research Section
```
[Brief description of your dissertation research] → Your dissertation
[Description of another research project] → Research project 1

[Research Interest 2] → Interest 2

[Research Interest 4] → Interest 4
```

### Publications Section

## Search and Replace Tips:

1. Open `index.html` in a text editor
2. Use Ctrl+F (Cmd+F on Mac) to find placeholders
3. Replace each placeholder with your actual information
4. Save the file and refresh your browser to see changes
