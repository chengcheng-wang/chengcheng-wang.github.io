About

I am a second-year PhD candidate in Linguistics at the LAnguages, Machines & Brains (LAMB) Lab, City University of Hong Kong, under the supervision of Prof. Jixing Li.

My research focuses on the interdisciplinary study of computational neurolinguistics that combines linguistics, neuroscience, and computer science to explore how the human brain and large language models process language. I am particularly interested in computational simulations of language disorders in the impaired brain.

Prior to joining the PhD program, I earned my M.A. in Language Studies from City University of Hong Kong and my B.A. in Japanese from Huazhong University of Science and Technology. I am fluent in Mandarin, English, and Japanese.
